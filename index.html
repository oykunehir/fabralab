Fabra Lab Uploader

Fabra Lab Uploader is an internal tool we use to send promotional videos for our own apps to our own TikTok account as drafts. Each video lands in the TikTok inbox, where we review it, write the final caption and publish it by hand inside the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not post on anyone else's behalf.
