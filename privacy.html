Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data Fabra Lab Uploader ("the tool") accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

When a TikTok account authorizes the tool, TikTok provides it with:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).
  • An access token and a refresh token that let the tool act within these permissions.

The tool does not read your videos, followers, messages, likes or any other TikTok data.

2. How data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the computer that runs the tool. They are not sent to any server operated by Fabra Lab or to any third party, apart from TikTok itself when uploading a video.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. The tool contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the account is disconnected. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, under Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the tool.

5. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by Fabra Lab.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the project's issue page.
